About the data. Lengths come from official agencies and TrailLink (each path lists its source). The highlighted lines follow hand-placed waypoints, so they show the general course but not every bend. Switch to the Cycling map layer to see the exact path drawn by OpenStreetMap. Elevations and grades are estimates: averages are reliable, but short underpass dips (often 4–8%) aren't shown. Metro station locations come from LA Metro's published station data. Stops and parking are well-known parks, lots and business districts on or within about a mile of each path. Hours, fees, closures and rain gate closures change, so check before you ride.

Class I means a fully separated, off-street paved path. Some paths still have street crossings, and those are noted. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
