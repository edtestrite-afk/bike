About the data. Lengths are from official agencies and TrailLink (sources are listed on each path). Route lines are drawn from hand-placed waypoints, so they show the general course but not every bend. Elevations and grades are estimated from waypoint elevations: averages are reliable, but short underpass dips (often 4–8%) aren't shown. Stops are well-known parks and business districts on or within about a mile of each path. Hours, closures and rain gate closures change, so check before you ride.

Class I means a fully separated, off-street paved path. Some paths still have street crossings, and those are noted.
